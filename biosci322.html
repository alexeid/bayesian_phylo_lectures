Phylogenetics and Coalescence module

These six lectures form Alexei Drummond's contribution to BIOSCI 322. They open with an overview of what computational evolutionary biology can do, then build from how phylogenies are estimated, through the coalescent and what it reveals about population history, to the relationship between gene trees and species trees.
